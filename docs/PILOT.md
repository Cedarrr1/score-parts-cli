# Pilot protocol for orchestra librarians

Recruit 10–15 librarians who routinely prepare individual parts. Ask each to bring one or more scores they are authorized to process. Do not collect source scores centrally unless the participant separately agrees.

## Session

1. Record their current process and time a representative MuseScore/manual delivery task.
2. Have the participant process the same score with the desktop app without coaching beyond the README.
3. Ask them to inspect first, middle, and final pages plus any flagged pages against the source score.
4. Record whether every expected part was produced, what the local checks found, what they found by hand, and whether the exported PDFs were usable.
5. If they choose online AI review, obtain separate consent for each selected page and record useful suggestions and false alarms.
6. Follow up after two weeks to learn whether they used the tool again and what prevented repeat use.

## Decision review

Review conversion completion, median time saved, repeat use, and missed critical issues. Do not count PDF generation alone as success. Keep the offline product usable even if AI suggestions are unavailable or unhelpful.
